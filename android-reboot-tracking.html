Android reboot tracking

Android does not keep a list of its own restarts, which is why tracking them takes an app. This topic now lives on one page, the Android Reboot Tracker & Uptime Report, so all the information is in one place.

RebootMonitor is a free Android app that logs every restart of the device, shows live uptime since the last reboot, and keeps a 12-month history. It works on its own, without an account, and the history stays on the device.
