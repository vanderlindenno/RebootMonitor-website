1. What the app does

RebootMonitor records when the device it is installed on was restarted, shows the current uptime and a reboot history of up to 12 months, and can remind you to restart the device after the number of days you choose. It also offers a home screen widget.

Do not rely on the app as the only monitoring for devices where a missed restart could cause harm.

We test the app on several Android versions, but there are thousands of device models, and device makers change Android in their own way. We cannot test them all. Please check for yourself that the app works as described on your device, for example by restarting it once and checking that the restart shows up.
