Android uptime monitoring

Uptime is the time since the last restart, and Android only shows it deep inside the settings. This topic now lives on one page, the Android Reboot Tracker & Uptime Report, so all the information is in one place.

RebootMonitor is a free Android app that logs every restart of the device, shows live uptime since the last reboot, and keeps a 12-month history. It works on its own, without an account, and the history stays on the device.
